Chartify✗

Turn any picture into a printable cross-stitch chart — symbol pages, a colour key, a shopping list and a finished preview, collated into one PDF booklet.

Runs entirely in this browser. Your picture is never uploaded anywhere.

2 · Size & threads

200

The height follows your picture's proportions. More stitches means finer detail — and a much bigger job.

60

The most a chart can use is 163 — one for every symbol in the alphabet. Fewer colours are quicker to stitch and cheaper to buy.

14-count

Stitches per inch of your aida or evenweave. Only changes the finished measurements, not the chart itself.

Print at actual size, with no scaling, so the squares come out the size they were designed to be.
